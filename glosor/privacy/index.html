Privacy Policy

Short version: Glosor stores your vocabulary data only on your device. Svektor AB never sees it.

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Applies to Glosor version 1.0 and later

Summary. No account. No server. Your vocabulary lists, study history and settings are stored entirely on your device. The only data Svektor AB can receive is an optional anonymous crash report (opt-in) and any e-mail you choose to send us.

1. Controller

Svektor AB, Sweden, organisation number 559594-3498.
Privacy contact: info@svektor.se

2. What the app stores — on your device only

Glosor stores the following data locally on your device. Svektor AB has no access to any of it.

  • Vocabulary lists — the words, translations, categories and notes you create or import.
  • Study history — session dates, scores and per-word performance used to weight future sessions.
  • Settings and preferences — language, notification schedule, AI service URL (but never your API key — see section 5).
  • Trial / purchase status — managed by Apple's StoreKit on the device; we never see your payment details.

3. No internet connection by default

Glosor works fully offline. It makes no network requests on its own. The only connections initiated by the app are the two optional features described in sections 4 and 5.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera / Photo library. Used if you choose to import vocabulary by photographing a book or whiteboard. Text recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. No image or text is sent to Svektor AB.
  • Microphone. Used if you choose to practise with voice input. Speech recognition runs on-device. No audio is recorded or sent to Svektor AB.
  • Local notifications. Used for optional daily study reminders and streak alerts. These contain no personal data and do not leave your device.

All permissions are requested only when you first try to use the relevant feature and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings.

5. AI import — your own API key, your own service

Glosor can optionally connect to an AI service (such as Google Gemini or OpenAI) to interpret pasted homework text. This feature works as follows:

  • You enter the URL and API key of your chosen AI service. The key is stored only in your device's keychain — Svektor AB never receives it.
  • When you use the AI import feature, the app sends the vocabulary text directly from your device to the AI service you configured. It does not pass through any server operated by Svektor AB.
  • That data transfer is governed by the privacy policy of the AI service you chose, not by this policy.
  • If you use Google Gemini with your free Google account, Gemini's standard terms apply.

6. Optional crash reporting

The app includes Sentry for crash reporting. This is off by default and only activated if you explicitly opt in via Settings → Crash reporting. If opted in:

  • Anonymous technical information about the crash (stack trace, device model, iOS version, app version) is sent to Sentry, Inc., USA.
  • No vocabulary content, no study data and no identifying information is included in crash reports.
  • You can opt out at any time in Settings. Crash reports that have already been sent cannot be recalled.
  • Sentry's privacy policy is available at sentry.io/privacy.

7. In-app purchase — handled by Apple

Glosor offers a 60-day free trial followed by a one-time purchase of approximately SEK 29. All payment processing, billing and purchase history are handled by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy. Svektor AB does not receive your payment details.

To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

8. Data backup and export

You can export a backup of your vocabulary data from within the app. The exported file is not encrypted. You are responsible for keeping it safe. The app does not send it anywhere automatically.

Your data may also be included in your iOS device backup (iCloud or local). The backup is handled by Apple according to Apple's terms.

9. What Svektor AB receives

Only what you choose to send:

  • E-mail to support@svektor.se or info@svektor.se. We see your e-mail address and your message. We use it to help you and keep it only while the matter is open.
  • Aggregated App Store statistics. Apple provides us with download counts and aggregated crash figures. These cannot identify individual users.
  • Crash reports via Sentry — only if you have opted in (see section 6).

10. Children

Glosor does not collect personal data, making it suitable for use by students of any age. The vocabulary content you enter is not visible to Svektor AB. No age verification is required.

11. Your rights under GDPR

Since Svektor AB normally holds no personal data about you, there is typically nothing to provide. For any support e-mail you have sent, you may request erasure by writing to info@svektor.se. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

12. Changes

If the app introduces a feature that changes how data is handled — for example connecting to a Svektor-operated server — this page will be updated first and the app's release notes will describe the change.
