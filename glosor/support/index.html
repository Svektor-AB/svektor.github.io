App Support

Having trouble? We're happy to help.

Contact us

Send an e-mail to support@svektor.se and describe what happened. We try to reply within two working days (Sweden time). The app and support are provided by Svektor AB, org. number 559594-3498, Sweden.

Tip: Include your iOS version and app version (found in the app under Settings → About) — it helps us reproduce issues faster.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple ID you used to purchase. You can refund within Apple's standard window (typically 90 days) without needing to contact us first.

Note: Glosor offers a 60-day free trial before any charge, so the trial period should be more than enough to decide if the app works for you.

Common questions

My vocabulary lists are gone after reinstalling the app

Glosor stores everything locally on your device — there is no cloud backup. If you delete the app, its data is deleted with it. We recommend exporting a backup before reinstalling: open the app → Settings → About → Export backup. You can then restore from that file after reinstalling.

Can I move my vocabulary lists to a new phone?

Yes. Export a backup on your old phone (Settings → About → Export backup) and save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or e-mail it to yourself. On your new phone, open that file and choose Glosor to import it.

How does the AI import work?

The AI import feature sends the text you paste to an AI service of your choosing (for example Google Gemini or OpenAI). You supply your own API key — it is stored only in your device's keychain and never sent to Svektor AB. The AI service interprets the text and returns structured vocabulary pairs that the app imports.

The app doesn't understand my homework format

Try the AI import feature — it handles free-form text much better than the plain-text parser. If you don't have an AI API key, copy the text into ChatGPT or Gemini and ask it to convert the content to a simple list in the format "word — translation", one per line, then paste the result into the app's plain-text importer.

How do I cancel the trial?

Glosor uses a non-renewing trial — it runs for 60 days and then simply stops. There is no recurring subscription to cancel. You will only be charged if you explicitly tap "Buy full version" inside the app.

My crash-reporting opt-in question

Crash reporting is opt-in and off by default. To change your preference, open Settings → About → Crash reporting inside the app. If opted in, only anonymous technical details (device model, iOS version, stack trace) are sent — no vocabulary content ever leaves your device this way.
