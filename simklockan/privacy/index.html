The short version

  • No account, no server, no tracking. Svektor AB does not receive, store or have access to the names, times or any other data you enter in the app.
  • Everything stays on your phone, in a database that is encrypted on the device.
  • The app never connects to the internet. On Android it does not even ask for that permission. There are no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting services in it.
  • The camera is used only to scan a QR code from another coach's phone. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • Data only leaves your phone when you choose to: showing a share QR code to another coach, or exporting a PDF.

1. Who we are

Swim Coach Timer is made by Svektor AB, organisation number 559594-3498, Sweden. You can reach us at info@svektor.se.

2. What the app stores — on your phone only

When you use the app, it keeps the following on your device. We never see any of it.

WhatDetails
SwimmersThe name or nickname you type for each swimmer, which group they belong to, and whether they are active.
Groups and training plansGroup names, training sessions and the sets in them (distance, stroke, repetitions, start pattern, notes you write), and lane assignments.
ResultsFinish times and lap splits, with the date, distance and stroke.

Please keep it minimal — and note that this is your responsibility. The app asks for a nickname; a first name or nickname is enough. The app cannot check what you type, so it is up to you (and your club) not to enter personal data you do not need: no surnames, personal identity numbers, health information, contact details or anything else that identifies a child.

3. Who is responsible for the swimmer data

Because the data lives only on your phone and Svektor AB never receives it, you, or the club or organisation you coach for, decide why and how the swimmers' data is used. In data-protection terms that makes you (or your club) the controller of that data. Svektor AB supplies the software only; we are neither the controller nor a processor of it, because we have no access to it.

In practice that means your club's own privacy notice should tell swimmers and their parents that names and times are recorded, why, with whom they may be shared (see section 6) and for how long they are kept. Clubs normally rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for ordinary training records. If you are unsure, ask your club's data-protection contact. We can send a short template wording on request.

4. Children

The app is for adult coaches and is not intended for use by children. Children never use the app; they appear in it only as a first name or nickname and swim times entered by their coach. We do not ask for dates of birth, photographs, addresses or contact details of anyone.

5. How your data is protected

  • The database is encrypted on the device (AES-256). The encryption key is generated on first launch and kept in the phone's secure key store (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). It never leaves the phone.
  • The app has no network access: there is no server to attack. On Android the app does not request the internet permission at all.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting libraries and writes no personal data to logs.
  • We recommend a passcode or biometric lock on the phone: it is what protects the data if the phone is lost.

6. Sharing with another coach (QR code)

You can hand a training session to another coach by showing a QR code on your screen, which the other coach scans with their phone. This happens directly between the two phones; nothing passes through a server. A code that shares a session contains the session's title, the sets in it (distance, stroke, repetitions and start settings), and for each swimmer their name and a random token. A code that sends results back contains only those tokens with the times and lap splits — no names. Tokens are random and new for each share. A shared name is pseudonymised, not anonymous.

A coach who receives a shared session becomes responsible for that copy. Received sessions are deleted from the app automatically once the results have been sent back, and can be deleted by hand at any time. Only share with coaches who need the information for the training.

7. Camera

The app asks for camera access only so that you can scan such a QR code. Images are processed on the phone, are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

8. PDF reports and the share menu

If you export a PDF, or use the phone's share menu, the file goes wherever you send it (for example by e-mail or to a printer). From that point it is outside the app and outside our control, so please consider who the recipient is.

9. Backups

  • iPhone / iPad: the app's data may be included in your iCloud or computer backup, as it is for most apps. The database stays encrypted in the backup. If you do not want this, turn off backup for the app in your phone's settings.
  • Android: the app is excluded from Android's cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. Its data therefore does not move to a new phone by itself.
  • Your own backup file: in Settings you can create a backup file of everything in the app and share it to a place you choose (Files, a drive, e-mail to yourself), and restore it later or on another phone. This file is not encrypted and contains swimmers' names and times, so you are responsible for keeping it safe and deleting it when no longer needed. The app itself never sends it anywhere.

10. How long data is kept, and deleting it

The app keeps your data until you delete it; Svektor AB has no copy to delete. In the app you can delete a single swimmer (with all of their results), a whole group, or all data at once (Settings). You can also delete a single result by swiping it away in a swimmer's history, and set the app to delete results older than 12, 24 or 36 months automatically (Settings). Deleting the app removes its data from your phone.

11. What Svektor AB does receive

Only what you choose to send us:

  • E-mail to info@svektor.se. We see your e-mail address, your message and anything attached. We use it to answer you and to fix problems (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f), and, where you ask us something, steps at your request). We keep support e-mail while the matter is open and delete it afterwards. Please do not send screenshots that show children's names; blur or crop them first.
  • Store statistics. Apple and Google give us aggregated figures about downloads and crashes. They are not personal data about you and we cannot tell users apart. Apple and Google handle your purchase and account data under their own policies.

We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising. E-mail to our addresses reaches us via Cloudflare (forwarding) and Google (Gmail), which may process it in the EU and the United States. We use your address and message only to reply and fix problems, keep it while the matter is open, and then delete it. Please don't put children's names or other personal data in a support message — we never need it. The website svektor.se is static: it uses no cookies, no login and no visitor analytics. Its pages are served via GitHub Pages, whose provider may temporarily log IP addresses for operational and security reasons; this is outside our control and we do not use those logs.

12. Your rights

For data that Svektor AB holds about you (such as a support e-mail) you have the right under the GDPR to access it, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its use, and receive a copy. Write to info@svektor.se. You may also complain to the Swedish supervisory authority, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

For swimmer data in the app, contact the coach or club that entered it: they are the controller, and only they can find, correct or delete it.

13. Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy — for example by adding an account or online storage — we will update this page first and say so in the app's release notes. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
