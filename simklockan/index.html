Swim Coach Timer

Timing for swim training · Simklockan in Swedish

For coaches who time a whole group at once

Put your swimmers in lanes, press START, and tap each swimmer's card as they finish. Plan the training session set by set, see every swimmer's personal bests and progress, and share a session with another coach by QR code. No account, no internet, no subscription tracking you: everything stays on your phone, encrypted.
