Privacy Policy

Short version: we do not collect personal data, and our apps store all data locally on your device.

Last updated: 31 August 2026

This policy covers the website svektor.se and the apps published by Svektor AB. An individual app may provide additional information on the App Store or Google Play; in the event of a conflict, the information shown for that specific app applies.

1. Data controller

Svektor AB, Sweden. Privacy contact: info@svektor.se.

2. The svektor.se website

  • The website is static and uses no cookies, no login and no visitor analytics.
  • The pages are served via GitHub Pages. The provider may temporarily log IP addresses for operational and security reasons. This is outside our control and we do not use these logs.

3. Our apps

  • All user data is stored locally on your device — for example content you create in the app, your settings and your history. None of it is sent to Svektor AB.
  • The apps require no account and have no login with us.
  • The apps contain no ads, no in-app purchases and no behavioural tracking.
  • If an app costs money or has a subscription fee, the payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play — not by us. We never receive your payment details.
  • Optional features that require the internet: an app may have optional features that call an external service. Such features are described in the app and, where applicable, use your own API key. Data you choose to send is then covered by that external service's terms, not by this policy.
  • Permissions (e.g. camera, microphone or photos) are used only for the feature you start yourself, and the content does not leave your device except when you explicitly choose to share it.

4. Email contact

If you contact info@svektor.se or support@svektor.se, we keep your message and email address for as long as needed to help you and follow up on the matter. We do not use the information for marketing and do not share it.

5. Children's privacy

If any of our apps is aimed at children, it still collects no data. We therefore do not process any personal data about children.

6. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to know what personal data we process about you and to request correction or erasure. Since we normally do not process any personal data, there is usually nothing to provide. For email correspondence you can request erasure by emailing info@svektor.se. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

7. Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new date above.
