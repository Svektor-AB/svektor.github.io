What we do

A small studio, focused apps

We build our own products from idea to release and maintain them over time. The work centres on a few areas where a well-considered app makes a real difference.

Education

Apps that help children and adults practise and learn at their own pace.

Finance & bookkeeping

Tools for personal finance and bookkeeping that keep the numbers on your own computer.

Productivity

Small, fast apps that solve one problem well — with no subscription.

How we build

Four principles

1

Local first

Your information is stored on your device, not with us. Most of our apps work entirely offline.

2

No tracking

No ads, no behavioural analytics, and no third-party tools collecting data about you.

3

Built to last

Test-driven development and careful review before every release.

4

Swedish and simple

Developed in Sweden, with clear language and a focus on accessibility.

Technology

One codebase, every platform

We build with Flutter and share a common graphic profile and codebase across platforms. That gives the same experience on phone, tablet and computer, and lets us spend our time on features rather than maintaining five separate apps.
