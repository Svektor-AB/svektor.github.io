Help & contact

Support

We're happy to help with our apps — in English or Swedish.

Contact us

Write to support@svektor.se. We'll get back to you as soon as we can, usually within a few business days.

Please include

  • Which app it concerns and the app version
  • Your device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 13, iOS 18)
  • A description of what happens and what you expected
  • A screenshot if it's something visible in the app

About your data: our apps store all information locally on your device. We therefore have no access to your data and cannot recover anything that has been lost. Back up regularly using the app's export function, especially before switching devices or uninstalling an app.

Bug reports and suggestions

Found a bug or have a request? Email support@svektor.se with the subject Bug or Suggestion. We read everything, even if we can't always reply to every suggestion.
